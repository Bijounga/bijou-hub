BijouHub

A desktop hub for focused work — launch a work mode, track time on projects, and plan the day's goals.

  • Modes open the apps you need and block the ones you don't.
  • Projects track goals and time, with script and music progress from BijouDocs and BijouMusic.
  • Timers count down from the app or a Stream Deck key.
  • Today's goals can sync through Google Tasks, so the same list is on your phone.
