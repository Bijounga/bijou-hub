Privacy Policy

Last updated October 2, 2026

BijouHub is a desktop app for planning and tracking your own work. It runs on your computer. There is no BijouHub server, account, analytics, or advertising.

Google Tasks

If you choose to connect Google Tasks, BijouHub asks Google for permission to manage your tasks (the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/tasks scope). It uses that permission only to:

  • show your open tasks (and those completed today), grouped into tabs by list name prefix, such as EDITING - …, STUDY - … and LIFE - …;
  • create, rename, complete, reopen, move and delete tasks when you do so in BijouHub;
  • create new lists when you link a goal to a project or ask for a new list.

BijouHub only changes tasks and lists when you act on them in the app, and doesn't access anything else in your Google account (no email, contacts, files or profile).

What is stored, and where

  • On your computer only: the sign-in token Google issues to BijouHub, encrypted with your Windows account, and a local copy of your goals so the app opens quickly and works offline.
  • In your Google account: the tasks themselves, exactly as you'd see them in the Google Tasks app.
  • Nowhere else. BijouHub never sends your data to the developer or any third party.

Google API Services User Data Policy

BijouHub's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google is used only to provide the task features described above, is never sold, never used for advertising, and never read by people.

Turning it off

Click Disconnect in BijouHub's Google Tasks window to sign out and delete the stored token. You can also remove BijouHub's access at any time from your Google Account permissions. Your tasks stay in Google Tasks either way.

Other data

Projects, session history, notes and settings are saved as files on your computer, or in a folder you choose to sync yourself (for example Google Drive or Dropbox). BijouHub checks GitHub for new versions of itself; that request contains no personal data.

Contact
